Balance: Mobile Number Puzzle

A calm number puzzle about hanging mobiles, for iPhone.

Common questions

How do I play? Drag numbered weights from the tray onto the empty hooks until every beam hangs level.

What is the rule? On each beam, add up weight × distance on the left and on the right. The two sides must be equal. A hanging beam weighs as much as everything on it. Balloons pull up, so they count as minus.

I hung the wrong weight. Tap it to take it off, drag it to another hook, or use undo.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your iPhone. Deleting the game deletes your progress.

Do I have to watch ads? No. Videos are optional and only give extra hints after the free ones are used.
