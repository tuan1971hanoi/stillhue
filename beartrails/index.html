Bear Trails: Path Puzzle

A cute timing puzzle about leading bears home to their hives, for iPhone.

Common questions

How do I play? Drag from each bear to the hive with its colour, then press Go. All the bears walk together, one square per beat.

Why did my bears bump? Two bears can't be on the same square or swap places. Send one the long way round.

What does a red number mean? On that beat the bear would fall in the river or meet a wild animal. Change the path before you press Go.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your iPhone. Deleting the game deletes your progress.

Do I have to watch ads? No. Videos are optional and only give extra hints after the free ones are used.

How do I turn the sound off? Open How to Play from the map. The sound switch is at the bottom.
