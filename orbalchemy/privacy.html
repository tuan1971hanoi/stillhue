Orb Alchemy Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Orb Alchemy ("the game", "we") is a colour-mixing puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (finished levels, stars, free hints left) and your sound setting on your iPhone. This information never leaves your device, is not sent to us, and is deleted when you delete the game. There are no accounts and we never ask for your name, email or contacts.

Advertising

The game is free and shows optional video ads (for example, to get an extra hint). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether the game may track you for personalised ads. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in these regions, the game shows Google's consent form before any ads load, and you can accept or refuse personalised ads.
  • Ads are optional: you can play every level without watching any ad.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data we receive

We, the developer, do not receive, sell or share any personal information about you. We may see anonymous totals from Google AdMob (such as how many ads were shown), which do not identify you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
