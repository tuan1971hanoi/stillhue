Common questions

How do I play? Swap tiles until the colors blend smoothly. A star shows a tile is in the right spot. Tap the "?" button in any level for the full rules.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your iPhone. Deleting the game deletes your progress.

Do I have to watch ads? No. Videos are optional and only give extra answer peeks or extra moves.
